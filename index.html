Our Story

Most protein
is mostly filler.

We started RS Wellness because we got tired of reading labels where the protein number was small and the rest of the bag was something we couldn’t pronounce.

So we did it backwards. A blend is only worth making if every ingredient on the label is doing real work. No “flavouring”, no proprietary carrier bulking out the weight, no sucralose, no maltodextrin, no amino spike.

Our base is yellow split pea and brown rice protein, both milled cold in small drums. The herbal half — moringa, ashwagandha, turmeric, tulsi — is dosed to traditional Ayurvedic practice levels, not to the highest number that still fits in a scoop.

02

Tested for what matters

Heavy metals, pesticide residue, microbial load and amino profile — every batch, independently.

03

Sweetened only by plants

Stevia leaf, monk fruit or our own jaggery. No sugar alcohols that upset your stomach.

What Goes In

Six ingredients we refuse to cut

These build every blend. Each one is bought whole, named on the label with its botanical, and never swapped for a cheaper substitute.

01

Pea protein

Pisum sativum

Our base. Complete amino profile, easy to digest, and it doesn’t taste like a vegetable when it dissolves.

02

Moringa

Moringa oleifera

Leaf only, shade-dried. We reject lots with woody stalk because the stem dilutes the active fraction.

03

Chia & flax

Salvia hispanica

Whole seed, ground in-house. Pre-ground chia goes rancid fast, so we mill it the same week we fill.

04

Ashwagandha

Withania somnifera

Root-only at a considered dose. Too much and it puts people off the whole range.

05

Brown rice

Oryza sativa

Balances pea’s amino profile and makes the blend mix clear instead of chalky.

Farm To Scoop

Five steps, none of them rushed

01

Source

Peas and rice from contract farmers in Madhya Pradesh and Punjab. Moringa from Tamil Nadu leaf cooperatives.

02

Mill cold

Roller-milled cool. Heat from a hammer mill denatures proteins and is why your shake tastes chalky.

03

Blend

Herbs dosed by weight on a calibrated scale, then triple-sieved so there are no clumps at the bottom of the jar.

04

Test

Independent lab for heavy metals, pesticides, microbes and amino acid profile. A failed batch is discarded.

05

Fill

Food-grade jars with a desiccant pack, nitrogen-flushed where needed. Batch code and milled-on date on every lid.
