Our Story

We never
wanted a factory.

What started as a single stone grinder in a Varanasi courtyard is now a small unit that still works the same way — one herb at a time, one batch at a time.

Every product we make is a single-plant or minimal-blend formula. If an ingredient doesn’t earn its place on the label, it doesn’t go in the jar. No synthetic fragrance, no mineral oil, no "herbal" blends that are 90% carrier oil.

We work directly with 120+ small farmers across Madhya Pradesh, Uttarakhand and Tamil Nadu, paying above market rate and buying whole — leaf, root, flower, seed.

01

Whole-plant sourcing

We buy raw herb, never extract. Extraction is where most brands quietly lose potency.

02

Lab-tested, every batch

Heavy metals, pesticide residue and microbial load checked before anything is filled.

03

Full label honesty

Botanical names, origin and exact percentage — printed, not hidden.

The Raw Material

Five herbs we keep coming back to

These are the plants that build most of our shelf. Every one of them is bought whole from a farm we’ve visited.

01

Tulsi

Ocimum sanctum

Respiratory and daily immunity. We buy the Krishna tulsi variety — higher eugenol, sharper taste.

02

Ashwagandha

Withania somnifera

Root grade only. We reject lots with visible root-rot and never accept leaf-inflated powder.

03

Neem

Azadirachta indica

Leaf and bark, bitter enough to be convincing. Sweetened neem powder usually means filler.

04

Bhringraj

Eclipta prostrata

Called the “king of hair” in Ayurveda. Fresh herb, dried within a day of picking.

05

Shallaki

Boswellia serrata

Guggulu resin graded for resin content. Higher grade means better results and a longer wash.

From Farm To Jar

Five steps, none of them fast

01

Source

We visit farms in person, three times a year. Contracts are written per-lot, not per-season.

02

Dry

Sun-dried in the open where possible. Anything shade-dried or machine-dried is marked on the label.

03

Grind

Stone-ground in small drums. Steel mills go faster but heat the powder, and heat costs you potency.

04

Test

Third-party lab for heavy metals, pesticides and microbes. A failed batch is discarded, not diluted.

05

Fill

Amber glass for oils, food-grade jars for powders. Batch number and harvest date on every lid.
